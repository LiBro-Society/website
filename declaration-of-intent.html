Declaration of Intent

← Back to Homepage

These are the promises we make to each other, and to anyone who joins us.

  1. We'll build our own way of helping each other beside the old system, without waiting for it to fix itself.
  2. We'll start today, with whatever we already have.
  3. We'll pass value straight from one person to the next, through a LiBro Voucher or just a helping hand, with no middleman taking a cut.
  4. We'll keep that value close to home, at the small stores and stalls people in our neighborhoods already go to.
  5. We'll keep our money away from anyone who holds too much power, in politics, business or religion, so corruption has less to feed on.
  6. Nobody will be put in charge of everyone else. Each LiBro Circle runs itself, and trust holds us together.
  7. We'll treat everyone as someone with something to give, never only as someone who needs help.
  8. We'll do all of this peacefully and in the open.

A lot of this has already started. The Living Manifesto explains why we're doing it.
