How it works

  1. I

    Help where you are

    Cook and share a meal. Bring what you can: an afternoon, an extra pair of hands. Some days you help, other days it's your turn to be helped.

  2. II

    Pass on a voucher

    A LiBro Voucher is paper with a QR code. Give it to someone who needs it, and they can pass it on to someone who needs it more. A LiBro partner nearby scans it, and LiBro pays the partner back. Each voucher works once.

  3. III

    Start a LiBro Circle

    Get a few neighbors together and do something useful in the same place, again and again. Nobody has to approve it. Here's how.
