Start a LiBro Circle

← Back to Homepage

A LiBro Circle is a few people looking after each other where they live. There's no sign-up and no approval. You can start one this week. Walang sign-up at walang approval. Puwede kang magsimula ngayong linggo.

1. Pick one place

Choose a place you can come back to: your street, a school, the barangay hall, a spot near the market. Then find a few people who live or work around it. A Circle grows where the same people see each other again and again.

2. Do one small, useful thing together

Cook a meal together, share what you have, or help a neighbor with something they're stuck on. Bring whatever you can, even if it's only an afternoon or an extra pair of hands. Everyone has something to give, and some days you're the one being helped.

3. Come back

Do it again, at the same place and around the same time. Once a week is plenty. When the same faces keep coming back, a one-time event becomes a Circle. You don't need a big launch. Do the thing first and talk about it after.

4. Give it a name

Call it LiBro Society plus your place, like LiBro Society Quezon City, or LiBro Society and your barangay or town. Keep the words LiBro Society so people know who you are. Nobody has to approve it, and nobody is put in charge of everyone else. Your Circle runs itself, and trust holds it together.

5. Vouchers

A Circle can't make its own vouchers, and a printed copy someone made themselves won't scan as valid. Vouchers come from LiBro. To get some for your Circle, message us on Telegram.

Give a voucher to someone who needs it. If they know someone who needs it more, they can pass it on, as long as it hasn't expired.

They use it at a LiBro partner: a store, a stall, or just someone who accepts it. The partner scans the QR code, hands over what the voucher is for, and LiBro pays the partner back.

Each voucher works once, so a photocopy or a screenshot won't get through. The scan screen shows the value and a "Valid until" date.

If a store or stall near you would like to be a partner, tell us on Telegram too.

6. Let your neighbors find you

After your Circle has met a few times, make a public page for it, like a Facebook page, with a name that includes LiBro Society. Post what you did and when you'll be there next, so people nearby can show up.

LiBro flyers carry a QR code that tells people more. Ask us on Telegram and print a few to hand out when you meet.

Not sure where to begin? Message us on Telegram, or talk to us here. Kung may tanong ka, kausapin mo lang kami.
