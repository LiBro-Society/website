We call it "living" because it grows as we do.

A lot of everyday life gets decided by people you'll never meet. A bank can freeze your account. The people who print money can shrink your savings, and nobody asks you first. LiBro Society is how ordinary people take some of that power back.

So we're building our own way of looking after each other, right beside the old system. Nobody at the top can switch it off, and nobody in the middle takes a cut.

There's no head office to ask. Anyone can start a LiBro Circle with the people around them, without waiting for approval. Just keep the name LiBro Society so people know who you are.

Value moves around the circle through the LiBro Voucher. It goes to someone who needs it, and if they know someone who needs it more, they can pass it on. It's used at a LiBro partner nearby, which can be a store, a stall or just someone who accepts it. The partner scans the QR code, hands over what the voucher is for, and LiBro pays them back. Each voucher works once, so a photocopy won't get past the scan. The value ends up with the people and small businesses around you, where a bank or a big corporation never touches it.

Corruption grows where money and power collect in the same few hands. It happens in government and in big business, and it happens in religious institutions too. Every voucher spent at a neighbor's stall is value those hands never get to hold, and the less they hold, the less there is to feed corruption.

Everyone here has something to give, even if it's just an afternoon or an extra pair of hands. Some days you're the one helping. Other days it's your turn to be helped.

What you say is yours, so we use tools that keep your conversations private.

People looked after each other long before there were banks. When a family had a job too big to manage alone, the neighbors showed up and did it with them. We do the same thing today, with vouchers and phones.

You don't fill out a form to join. Help someone where you are, or pass on a voucher if you have one. If a few neighbors are up for it, start a Circle together. It doesn't matter when you showed up or how much you have.

If you want to start a Circle, here's how. Or just talk to us.
